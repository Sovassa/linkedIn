# Sovassa LinkedIn CTA Rules

## Discussion CTA

Use when the objective is conversation.

Examples:

- What has worked for you?
- Have you experienced this?
- What's your biggest challenge here?

## Save CTA

Use for educational content.

Examples:

- Save this checklist for your next audit.
- Keep this framework handy.

## Share CTA

Use only when the content genuinely deserves distribution.

## Soft Business CTA

Use for relevant business topics.

Examples:

- If you're reviewing your digital strategy, this is worth checking first.
- If you're facing this problem, a structured audit is usually the right starting point.

## Rules

Do not end every post with:

"Contact Sovassa today."

Do not use aggressive sales CTAs.

The CTA should match the purpose of the post.
