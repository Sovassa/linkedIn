# Sovassa LinkedIn — Avoid

Never publish:

- Fabricated statistics
- Fabricated case studies
- Fabricated client results
- Fake testimonials
- Unverified claims
- Confidential client information
- Internal company information
- Generic motivational quotes without strategic purpose
- Excessive promotional posts
- Spammy engagement bait
- Competitor content copied or lightly rewritten
- Misleading claims
- Unsupported industry claims
- AI-generated filler
- Excessive emojis
- Hashtag stuffing

Avoid repetitive openings.

Avoid using the same post structure repeatedly.

Avoid publishing purely because a posting slot exists.
