# Sovassa LinkedIn Content Rules

## Rule 1

Every post must have a clear purpose.

## Rule 2

Avoid creating content purely to fill the calendar.

## Rule 3

Prefer practical information over generic advice.

## Rule 4

Use real Sovassa experience whenever available.

## Rule 5

Never fabricate:

- Statistics
- Client results
- Case studies
- Testimonials
- Experiences
- Project information

## Rule 6

Don't make every post promotional.

## Rule 7

Educational content should be the majority of the feed.

## Rule 8

Use examples whenever possible.

## Rule 9

Use credible sources for current industry claims.

## Rule 10

Don't copy competitors.

## Rule 11

Don't publish confidential client information.

## Rule 12

Don't mention a client or project publicly unless the information is approved for public use.

## Rule 13

Posts should be readable on mobile.

## Rule 14

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

## Rule 15

Don't use hashtags just for volume.
