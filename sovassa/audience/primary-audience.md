# Sovassa LinkedIn Target Audience

## Primary Audience

### 1. Business Owners

People who need help improving:

- Online visibility
- Lead generation
- Website performance
- Digital marketing
- Customer acquisition

### 2. Startup Founders

Businesses building their initial digital presence.

### 3. SME Owners

Businesses looking to improve their existing digital marketing.

### 4. Marketing Managers

Professionals responsible for:

- SEO
- Paid advertising
- Content
- Lead generation
- Digital strategy

### 5. Growth Managers

Professionals focused on:

- Acquisition
- Conversion
- Performance
- Growth

## Secondary Audience

- Entrepreneurs
- Technology decision makers
- Product managers
- E-commerce businesses
- Marketing professionals
- Startup teams

## Audience Pain Points

Common problems include:

- Poor online visibility
- Low-quality leads
- High advertising costs
- Poor SEO
- Slow websites
- Weak websites
- Poor conversion rates
- Lack of digital strategy
- Inconsistent content
- Poor marketing measurement
- Lack of technical expertise
