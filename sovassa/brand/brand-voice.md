# Sovassa LinkedIn Brand Voice

## Voice

Sovassa should sound:

- Clear
- Confident
- Professional
- Practical
- Modern
- Approachable
- Business-focused
- Knowledgeable

## Writing Style

Prefer:

- Short paragraphs
- Clear explanations
- Concrete examples
- Real numbers when available
- Practical recommendations
- Specific observations
- Natural language

Avoid:

- Excessive corporate language
- Generic motivational content
- Fake enthusiasm
- Overly promotional language
- Unsupported claims
- Artificial statistics
- Clickbait
- Excessive emojis
- Excessive hashtags

## AI Writing Restrictions

Avoid generic AI phrases such as:

"In today's digital landscape"

"Unlock the power of"

"Game-changing"

"Revolutionize your business"

"Take your business to the next level"

"10X your business"

"Transform your business"

"Cutting-edge solutions"

Use specific language instead.

## Brand Personality

The reader should feel that the post was written by an experienced digital business team, not by a generic AI content generator.
