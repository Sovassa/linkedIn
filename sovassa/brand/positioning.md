# Sovassa LinkedIn Positioning

## Core Position

Sovassa is a digital marketing and technology partner that helps businesses turn their digital presence into a practical growth channel.

## What We Talk About

Sovassa LinkedIn content should focus on:

- Digital marketing
- SEO
- Paid advertising
- Websites
- Technology
- AI
- Business growth
- Digital transformation
- Real project experiences
- Marketing lessons
- Practical business advice

## What We Want To Be Known For

Sovassa should gradually become associated with:

1. Practical digital marketing knowledge
2. SEO expertise
3. Performance-focused advertising
4. Strong web development
5. Practical technology solutions
6. Business-focused digital strategy

## Positioning Principle

Teach first.

Demonstrate expertise second.

Sell only when relevant.

## Content Philosophy

Every post should ideally provide at least one of:

- A useful insight
- A practical lesson
- A new perspective
- A solution to a common problem
- Evidence from real experience
- A useful framework
- A meaningful industry observation
